Master grammar connections with Web Speech audio support

1. ても (Even if / Even though)

Used primarily with Verbs (て-form) and い-Adjectives (くて-form).

難しくてもやります。
Even if it's difficult, I will do it.
雨が降っても行きます。
Even if it rains, I will go.

2. でも (Concession for Nouns & な-Adjectives)

Used with Nouns and な-Adjectives to express "even if..."

嫌いでも食べなさい。
Even if you dislike it, eat it.

3. でも (Suggestions / Examples)

Used after Nouns to suggest an option ("...or something / or the like").

パンでも食べる。
I'll eat a sandwich or something.
ケーキでも構いません。
Cake is fine as well.
