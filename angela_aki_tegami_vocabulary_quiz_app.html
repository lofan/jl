Letter to My 15-Year-Old Self

"Tegami ~Haikei Jugo no Kimi e~" is a celebrated Japanese ballad structured as a poignant dialogue between a troubled 15-year-old writing to their future self, and the adult self offering encouragement, resilience, and hope across time.
