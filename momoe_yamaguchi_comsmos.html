Song Overview

秋桜 (Cosmos)

Composed by Masashi Sada and famously performed by Momoe Yamaguchi in 1977, "Cosmos" is a touching Japanese folk masterpiece. It expresses a daughter's heartfelt gratitude and mixed emotions towards her mother on the autumn eve before her wedding.

Sample Lyric Excerpt

「淡紅の秋桜が秋の日の 何気ない陽溜りに揺れている」

"Pale pink cosmos flowers sway gently in the casual sunny warmth of an autumn day..."
