Song Overview & Theme

"Inochi no Namae" is the iconic vocal piece from Miyazaki's masterpiece Spirited Away. Its poetry explores nostalgic memories, forgotten bonds, the flow of time, and the deep, silent connection between living souls.

「青空に線を引く ひこうき雲の白さは…」
"The whiteness of the vapor trail drawing a line in the blue sky..."
