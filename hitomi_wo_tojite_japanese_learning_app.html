瞳をとじて (Hitomi wo Tojite)

"With My Eyes Closed" — Released 2004

Ken Hirai's iconic ballad explores deep nostalgia, longing, and eternal affection following loss. Through evocative imagery—shadows, light, seasons, and memories—the lyrics depict how love persists inside one's heart even when someone is gone.

Song Theme Quote:
